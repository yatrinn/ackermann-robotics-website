2. In short

This website sets no cookies. For anonymous audience measurement we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, and we use Leadfeeder to see which companies visit our website, in both cases without cookies and without recognising individual visitors. Fonts and images are loaded from our own server. We only process personal data when you contact us through the form or by email.

3. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time of access and browser information to deliver the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of the website. Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses. DNS resolution for our domain is provided by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA).

4. Audience measurement

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) to see how often our pages are visited. When you load a page, your browser loads a small script from Cloudflare that transmits the page visited, the referring page, browser and device type, load times and the country derived from your IP address. No cookies are set, nothing is stored on your device and no profiles are created; individual visitors are not recognised. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is measuring reach and technically improving the website. Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

We also use Leadfeeder by Dealfront Group GmbH and its affiliates, in particular Dealfront Finland Oy (EU), as a processor. Leadfeeder matches the shortened IP address of your visit against publicly available company data to determine which company visited our website, and shows us which pages were viewed and when. We only see the company, not you as a person. No cookies are set. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is recognising business interest in our services. You can object at any time by emailing info@ackermann-robotics.org.

5. Demo requests through the form

When you request a demo through our form, we process your name, work email, company, optionally your phone number, and message to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract). The form is delivered by Web3Forms, operated by Web3Creative (India), acting as our processor. Web3Forms forwards your request to us by email. The transfer to a country outside the EU is based on the provider's data processing agreement.

6. Contact by email

When you email us, we process your details to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Our mailboxes are hosted by Zoho Mail (Zoho Corporation B.V., Netherlands) in EU data centres.

7. Our emails to companies

We write to companies where we expect a need for support in production, for example because they have advertised a matching position. For this we process business contact details (name, business email address, company, role) and details from the job advertisement. This data comes from publicly accessible sources such as job advertisements and company websites, or from personal contacts, for example at trade fairs. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is initiating business relationships.

Our emails contain a personal link. If you open it, we store which email the link belongs to, the time, the country derived from your IP address and your browser identifier. We do not store the IP address itself and set no cookies. If you fill in the form on the linked page, we store your answers. If you reply to an email, we store sender, recipient, subject, text and time to handle your request.

To draft our emails and analyse replies we use the AI service of Anthropic PBC (USA) as a processor. We send and receive these emails through our mailboxes at Zoho Mail (Zoho Corporation B.V., Netherlands, EU data centres). Link handling and hosting of these functions are provided by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

You can object to this processing at any time (Art. 21(2) GDPR), via the unsubscribe link in every email or by replying. We then add your address to a suppression list so you receive no further emails.

8. Retention

We delete your data once it is no longer needed for the purpose. Data from our emails to companies is deleted no later than 12 months after the last contact unless a business relationship results. After an objection we keep the suppression list permanently so that we do not contact you again. If a business relationship results, we keep records for the statutory retention periods (up to 10 years).

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). Write to info@ackermann-robotics.org.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.
