FAQ.

How is this different from a normal industrial robot?

A conventional industrial robot follows fixed, programmed paths: every part must sit exactly in place, every variant is reprogrammed. Our robot uses cameras to see where the part is and learns by demonstration.

How does the robot learn our task?

Your team does the task a few times with a handheld gripper. The robot learns from that and from every correction by our operator.

What if we already have a robot?

Even better. We integrate it and add cameras and sensors.

What if the robot breaks or gets stuck?

Our operator takes over remotely during your production hours. Repairs and spare parts are included.

Our products and boxes change. Does that work?

Yes. It learns new variants by demonstration. Retraining is included.

Do we need robotics experts?

No. We install, run and maintain the robot.

Is it safe for our workers?

We set it up to machine safety rules and do the risk assessment with you before it starts.

What if it does not work for our task?

Then you pay nothing.
