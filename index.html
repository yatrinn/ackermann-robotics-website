Questions we get.

How does the robot learn our task?

Your team shows the task a few times, by hand with a handheld gripper. The robot learns from these demonstrations and from every correction by our operator, until it runs reliably.

What if we already have a robot?

Even better. We integrate your existing robot and add the cameras and other sensors your task needs. Then it learns the task just like ours.

What if the robot breaks or gets stuck?

Our operator takes over remotely, around the clock. Repairs and spare parts are part of the fee.

Our products and boxes change. Does that work?

Yes. The robot learns new variants from demonstration. Retraining is part of the service.

Do we need robotics experts?

No. We install, run and maintain the robot. Your team only shows us the task.

Is it safe for our workers?

We set the robot up according to the applicable machine safety rules and do the risk assessment with you before it starts.

What if it does not work for our task?

Then you pay nothing. The fee only starts once the robot does the task reliably.
